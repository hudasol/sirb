# Sirb report

Sirb is a simulation and planning tool. It is not flight-certified, makes no claim of regulatory
compliance, and has no flight data behind it. A real show needs an operator safety case and approvals
from the relevant UAE aviation authorities. Exports are files only, labeled "simulation-validated only".

## What was built
Procedural shape sequences become multi drone trajectories (polynomial segments, minimum jerk legs,
Hungarian assignment, three planners). An exact verifier solves minimum pairwise separation and the
dynamic limits for the trajectories as written. A Monte Carlo simulator then applies wind, GNSS
degradation, comms loss, low battery and motor failure, with contingency behaviours, and reports
violation probabilities with Wilson intervals. A viewer shows nominal and stressed runs side by side
in English and Arabic. Method details and every assumption: `docs/METHODS.md`.

## Three kinds of statement, kept apart
1. **Mathematically guaranteed, under the model.** For the polynomials as written, with drones as points,
   the verifier's minimum separation and limit checks are exact up to floating point and root finding
   tolerance (`docs/VERIFIER.md`). The bound is checked by a second implementation (`docs/VERIFIER.md`,
   differential section) and by mutation testing (`docs/MUTATION.md`). That is evidence, not a proof of
   the implementation.
2. **Only simulated.** Every stressed probability depends on assumed disturbance and tracking models.
   They are not measurements and not real world risk.
3. **Only planned.** Anything in `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md`, `docs/PROPOSAL_SEED.md` and the exploratory ground
   robot study is not an achievement.

## What the data say, plainly
- The demo shows verify, but their nominal margin over the 2.0 m threshold is small. In the simulated
  model modest GNSS bias breaks that margin long before wind does.
- Holding or descending in place after an event is the worst tested behaviour in a dense formation,
  because the rest of the fleet keeps flying through the held position; leaving along a clear corridor
  does better, and letting a comms loss drone continue its plan did best in that case. These are results
  for one 100 drone show under assumed models.
- The stronger planners did not add capability on the demo shows (`results/planners.json`).

## Sim to real gap
See `docs/METHODS.md` section 9. In short: no real flight data, generic tracking loop, no downwash, an
aircraft turbulence model, independent per drone GNSS errors, and no human or procedure factors.

## Reproduce
`make reproduce` (fixed seeds). Wall clock fields differ by machine.
