## How to read this

The exact and interval verifiers made no wrong verdict in any stratum. A sampled check matches them on gross and safe cases at every frame step tested, loses a small share of shallow violations at 0.5 s and 0.2 s, and misses most razor thin violations at every step, because a frame has to land within the violation depth of the closest approach. At the same wall clock time the sampler's step is finer than 0.03 s, and there it catches every shallow violation. The practical advantage of the exact verifier on these sets is therefore the guarantee at any depth, not a detection gain on violations deep enough to matter. Whether a 0.1 mm violation matters is a modelling question: the tracking error of a real vehicle is far larger.
