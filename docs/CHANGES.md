## Phase 6, stream E: deterministic targets and exact assignment costs

What changed: `sirb.shapes.generate_points` snaps every target to a 2^-20 m grid; `sirb.assign` computes Hungarian costs as exact integers on that grid; `sirb.planner.common` builds legs with products and `math.sqrt` instead of BLAS and `pow`. Reason and evidence: `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. Targets move by at most half a grid cell per coordinate, leg coefficients change in the last bits, and tied assignments of grid and text shapes may resolve to another member of the tie, which can change a plan substantially while keeping its total cost. Every fleet hash changed. All files below were regenerated on Linux by `scripts/run_phase6_chain.sh` (or the script named), with the same seeds and trial counts as before.
