# Changes that move published numbers

Every change below altered code whose output appears in `results/*.json`, the README or the docs.
The affected files were regenerated by their scripts; nothing was edited by hand.

## Phase 6

### Deterministic targets and exact assignment costs (stream E)

What changed: `sirb.shapes.generate_points` snaps every target to a 2^-20 m grid; `sirb.assign`
computes Hungarian costs as exact integers on that grid; `sirb.planner.common` builds legs with
products and `math.sqrt` instead of BLAS and `pow`. Reason and evidence: `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.

Effect: targets move by at most about 0.5 micrometre per coordinate; leg coefficients change in
the last bits; tied assignments of grid and text shapes may resolve to another member of the tie,
which can change a plan substantially while keeping its total cost. All fleet hashes changed.
Regenerated so far, on Linux with `scripts/run_phase6_chain.sh`: `viewer/data/` (all three
shows), `results/planners.json`, `results/assignment.json`. The text show's stressed scenario
minimum distances change visibly because its tied assignments resolve to another member of the tie.

**Not yet regenerated** at the time of writing (they still come from the pre phase 6 code):
`results/stress_sweeps.json`, `results/stress_spacing.json`, `results/stress_contingency.json`,
`results/stress_scaling.json`, `results/sensitivity.json`, `results/scaling_profile.json`,
`results/differential.json` (random fleets, not planned shows, so not expected to change) and the
README and `docs/RESULTS.md` lines derived from them. The chain regenerates the first five; the
owner's steps are in `docs/OVERNIGHT_REPORT.md`.
